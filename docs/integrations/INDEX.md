## Cross-cutting risks

1. **Webhook durability differs sharply**: Razorpay disables after 24 h; Cashfree default ~42 min and no replay for subscription webhooks; Stripe 3 days; Meta 36 h; Exotel unknown/unsigned. Nirantar needs an inbox + reconciliation pollers per provider.
2. **Idempotency gaps**: only Stripe (`Idempotency-Key`) and Cashfree (`x-idempotency-key`) offer request idempotency; Razorpay (refund `receipt` only), Exotel, WhatsApp, LLM/speech APIs require Nirantar-side dedupe.
3. **Stripe India** is invite-only — cannot be the primary India rail.
4. **Model lifecycle**: Haiku 4.5 retirement floor 2026-10-15; Sarvam-M/30B already deprecated; Groq preview models non-production.
5. **Regulatory**: UPI AutoPay limits and Collect deprecation, RBI pre-debit notices, TRAI DLT for SMS, WhatsApp opt-in, and unverified voice-collection rules — require legal review before launch.
